Privacy

This page explains what we collect when you buy from the Finality SMP store.

What we collect

  • Your Minecraft username (and, if available, its UUID) so we can deliver your items.
  • Your email address and country, provided by Stripe, so we can help with problems and keep tax records.
  • What you bought and when.

What we don't collect

We never see or store your card or bank details. Stripe handles payments; see Stripe's privacy policy.

What's shown publicly

The store may show your Minecraft username and what you bought under "Recent purchases" or "Top supporter". Your email and the amount you spent are never shown. Ask us on Discord if you'd like to be hidden.

Third parties

Player heads come from mc-heads.net, and the player count comes from mcsrvstat.us. Your browser contacts these sites directly when you load the store.

Your browser

The store remembers your username in your browser's local storage so you don't have to type it again. We don't use tracking cookies.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of your data or ask us to delete it by contacting us on Discord. We keep payment records as long as the law requires.
