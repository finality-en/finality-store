Frequently asked questions

How do I buy something?

Press Log in at the top of the store and enter your Minecraft username, then pick a package and continue to checkout. You pay on Stripe's secure checkout page, then return here.

How long does delivery take?

Usually under a minute. If you're offline, your items wait and are delivered automatically when you next join. Bank payments are delivered once the bank confirms them.

How do crate keys and lootboxes arrive?

They go straight into your inventory, so you need to be online with a free slot. If you are offline when you pay, they are delivered the next time you join. Use keys on the crates at spawn, and right-click a lootbox to open it.

How do I use kill effects and kill sounds?

Bundles and the Complete Collection unlock straight away. Type /ke in game and pick the effect or sound you want to equip. They are cosmetic only and change nothing about combat.

I play on Bedrock. Can I buy?

Yes. Choose Bedrock and enter your Xbox gamertag exactly as it appears in game, including spaces.

I typed the wrong username.

Open a ticket on our Discord with the email you paid with and the correct username. We'll move the purchase for you.

How do rank upgrades work?

If you already own a rank, buy the upgrade package and you only pay the difference. Upgrades are checked against your purchase history before they're delivered.

How do monthly ranks work?

Monthly ranks renew automatically each month. Cancel any time from the link in your Stripe receipt email. Your rank stays until the end of the period you've paid for.

How do gift cards work?

After buying a gift card, its owner receives a single-use code in-game. Enter it in the "Add promotion code" box at checkout.

Which payment methods can I use?

Cards, Apple Pay, Google Pay, PayPal and bank payments, depending on your country. You'll see the options available to you at checkout.

Is it safe?

Payments are handled entirely by Stripe, a payment provider used by millions of businesses. Your card details never touch our servers.
